SDG data made simple — and free to use

ISDM DataShakti brings structured and unstructured SDG data into one searchable, easy-to-use platform.

Finding reliable data for social impact work can be frustrating. Scraping government websites, paying for reports, or hiring analysts takes time, effort, and money.

ISDM DataShakti, powered by Capgemini, changes that. It is a free-to-use platform that offers SDG data—both structured and unstructured—in one place. It is simple to use and easy to search for the data you need.

With ISDM DataShakti, you can:

  • Access SDG data across education, health, gender, climate, and more
  • Search quickly using keywords, SDGs, or natural language
  • Visualise insights without needing technical skills
  • Share findings and co-create knowledge

Save time and focus on what really matters: creating impact.
